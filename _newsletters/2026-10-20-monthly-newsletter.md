#### 2026 SNAP Congressional District Visits

Year two of our 2026 SNAP Congressional District Visits initiative was a big success! We trained 115 early-career scientists who held 65 meetings with Congressional offices across 26 states. Notably, 71% of these meetings were held with the House offices that voted on a [C.R. mentioning the proposed OMB rule changes](https://www.naco.org/news/congress-passes-short-term-funding-bill-keeping-federal-government-funded-through-dec-11-and) shortly after returning from August recess.

Due to the time-sensitive nature of scheduling visits, **we recognize not everyone had a chance to participate in the initiative despite high interest.**

In light of this, **we are encouraging people to write and publish Op-Eds** to continue this conversation with the public about the benefits of fundamental research, the potential implications of rules like OMB-2026-0034, and the importance of prioritizing both accountability and stability of funding.

**If you are interested and have not yet filled out [this op-ed interest form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfeRmD1YYFLeErNy80NA7SQ-Jc3cPGUOO1LSG5NINI560WXLA/viewform?usp=header), please do!** Upon filling out this form, we will provide additional resources to help you write, including 1) advice from the former New York Times Opinion Editor Clay Risen, 2) SNAP guides to writing an Op-Ed and 3) assistance with editing and/or help pitching to outlets.

We are proud to announce that earlier this month we launched SciPol 101!
