Science Policy Courses

SNAP offers a modular science policy curriculum covering five topics: What is Science Policy?, How Government Works, Science Communication, Writing for Science Policy, and Science Diplomacy. These resources can be used to educate students at our home institutions and encourage the creation of new university science policy groups.

Before using these modules

  • These slide decks are view only. Download or copy to make changes.

  • Please keep the SNAP acknowledgements slide when presenting, sharing, or altering slides for further distribution, or credit SNAP when borrowing slides for other presentations.
